The apps moved inside Claude: Adobe, Canva and Notion open beside the chat

By inicIA · November 15, 2026 · 6 min read

Direct answer

On November 10, 2026, Anthropic expanded its interactive connectors: a connected app, such as Adobe, Canva, Notion or Asana, opens in full inside Claude, beside the chat or full screen, and Claude edits the page, row or file you have open while you keep typing. The launch covers thirteen apps: Adobe, Asana, Atlassian Confluence, Box, Canva, Carta, Dropbox, HubSpot, monday.com, Notion, Smartsheet, Vercel and Webflow. Figma, Harvey and Shopify are building theirs. The foundation is MCP Apps, the open extension of the Model Context Protocol. The rollout reaches the web and the Claude desktop app, on all plans, as each account receives the new Claude experience.

What changed from the January connectors

  • From a small card to the full app: Anthropic launched interactive connectors on January 26, 2026, with a small live view inside the conversation, with Amplitude, Asana, Box, Canva, Clay, Figma, Hex, monday.com and Slack. On November 10, the card becomes the full tool.
  • Live editing with approval: you ask Claude to change a page or a row, approve it like any connector action, and watch the change land in the app while you keep typing. The work stops being a text report and becomes a direct edit.
  • The app runs on your session: the app opens with your own account, your role and your permissions inside your workspace. Claude works within the limits your account already has.
  • Mobile stays out for now: the full view reaches the web and the desktop app. On iOS and Android, Claude keeps showing the older inline cards.

What Claude does inside each app

  • Adobe: retouches photos, edits and adds effects to images, and edits and redacts PDFs, according to the announcement.
  • Canva: creates and edits designs inside Claude, with your Canva account connected once.
  • Webflow: builds pages, components and CMS items, adds animation and answers traffic questions, inside your workspace.
  • Notion and Confluence: open the full editor with your session, to edit pages with Claude beside you.
  • Box: compares two versions of a file, according to the announcement.
  • Purchases stay out: Anthropic says purchases from views inside the chat stay outside the feature.

What it means for your business

  • The work window changed hands: the assistant was the place where you asked, and the app was the place where you worked. With interactive connectors, the app comes to the assistant. The fight between the labs becomes who owns the window where you work.
  • The standard is open: MCP Apps is an open extension of the Model Context Protocol, and Anthropic says it works in any AI app that supports it. A developer builds once and its app runs in more than one assistant.
  • Permissions matter more than ever: Claude edits with your session and your role. Reviewing roles and permissions in each connected app becomes part of your AI governance: who can edit what, and with which approval.
  • The developer side stays in public preview: app tools and embedded sites for custom connectors are in public preview, with access limited to a set of organizations. The end-user feature rolls out by account; the feature for building on top of it opens in phases.
  • The connector market is growing: Anthropic says its marketplace lists more than 9,000 connectors and plugins. The question for your business stops being which app to connect and becomes which complete workflow lives inside the chat.

Note: this article summarizes Anthropic's announcement of November 10, 2026, "Your apps can now open in Claude, and Claude can work in them", and coverage by Creative AI News, aicodex, Bits on Bots and GenAI Brief from November 10 to 12, 2026. Interactive connectors launched on January 26, 2026. The full-view rollout reaches accounts gradually, on the web and in the desktop app; the exact date per account varies.
