Oracle put ChatGPT inside its applications: what changes for your company, and what is still in development

By inicIA · October 29, 2026 · 6 min read

Direct answer

On October 27, 2026, at Oracle AI World in Las Vegas, Oracle and OpenAI announced an expanded alliance bringing ChatGPT and Codex to Oracle's applications, data and cloud infrastructure. The offering includes four pieces: Oracle AI Workspace, Oracle AI Security Mesh, Oracle App Composer and an Oracle AI SMB Bundle for small and midsize businesses. The central nuance: AI Workspace is still in development, per OpenAI itself, so the announcement describes a product direction, not general availability of everything announced.

The four pieces, without hype

  • Oracle AI Workspace: connects Fusion Applications, NetSuite, Oracle AI Database and Oracle Cloud Infrastructure with ChatGPT through plugins, to find information and complete approved workflows in finance, procurement and planning, under company permissions. Oracle says it keeps existing governance rules, approval chains and access controls intact.
  • Oracle AI Security Mesh: tools to find and fix vulnerabilities in systems, with OpenAI models aimed at cybersecurity.
  • Oracle App Composer: build applications connected to approved Oracle data with coding tools such as Codex, with deployment managed by Oracle on its cloud.
  • Oracle AI SMB Bundle: a package for approved small businesses combining ChatGPT Business, Codex, Oracle applications and Oracle Cloud Infrastructure credits. Per the product page cited in coverage, it includes $12,000 a year in OCI credits, renewable for up to two additional years, plus a one-time ChatGPT credit of $50 per user for up to five users. Availability is limited and requires approval, and each benefit carries its own terms.

The internal numbers Oracle did disclose

  • 130,000 active ChatGPT users inside Oracle and more than 95,000 active Codex users, per OpenAI. Those are company figures, describing internal adoption, not customer results.
  • Buying in the United States through Marketplace: Oracle customers in the United States can buy access to the OpenAI API, Codex and ChatGPT Work through Oracle Marketplace, using existing procurement processes and eligible Oracle Universal Credits. That buying route is specific to the United States in the announcement and should not be assumed identical elsewhere.
  • Dates and prices may change: Oracle itself warns that development schedules and pricing may change. Before budgeting, ask for the current terms for your case.

What it means for your business

  • Chat reaches the system where the money lives: the difference from a general chatbot is that here the assistant operates on your business applications, with permissions and approvals. That closeness is the value and also the risk: a misconfigured permission becomes a real action.
  • The question that governs: who can approve what, what gets logged, and who can revoke access within minutes. If your vendor cannot answer those three questions with evidence, the integration is not ready for production yet.
  • For a small business: the credit bundle lowers the entry barrier, but credits do not remove the work of organizing data, permissions and processes. AI over messy data delivers fast answers with the same original disorder.

Frequently asked questions

Is Oracle AI Workspace generally available?

Not generally. OpenAI says it is still in development. The October 27, 2026 announcement describes the product and its proposed function, not general availability.

What does the small-business bundle include?

It combines ChatGPT Business, Codex, Oracle applications and cloud credits. Per the product page, it includes $12,000 a year in OCI credits, renewable for up to two additional years, with limited availability and approval required.

How do customers buy in the United States?

Oracle customers in the United States can buy access to the OpenAI API, Codex and ChatGPT Work through Oracle Marketplace, with existing procurement processes and eligible Universal Credits.

What should I check before connecting ChatGPT to my applications?

Role-based permissions, approval chains, logs of every action, and fast access revocation. The integration is worthwhile when existing governance stays intact inside the chat.
