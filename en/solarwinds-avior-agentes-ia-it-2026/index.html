SolarWinds Launched Avior: Goal-Driven AI Agents for Enterprise IT | inicIA

By inicIA · October 14, 2026 · 6 min read

Direct answer

On October 14, 2026, SolarWinds unveiled Avior, a standalone agentic AI system designed for mission-critical IT environments. The pitch: goal-oriented agents that define objectives, coordinate specialized agents, and measure progress toward concrete outcomes across hybrid digital ecosystems — from advanced root-cause analysis to capacity, cost, and asset-lifecycle management. It is available starting October 14 through a guided onboarding program. The pattern it proposes transfers to any business: automate with clear goals, guardrails, and control, instead of unleashing agents to chase alerts.

What Avior does

  • Goal-oriented agents: they define objectives, pursue measurable outcomes, and coordinate specialized agents across the estate.
  • Root-cause diagnosis: analyzes issues from the operator's perspective, with deep dives whose findings can be shared with the team.
  • Capacity and cost management: tracks utilization and spend against the headroom the team wants to keep, flagging problems before they become incidents or invoices.
  • Asset lifecycle: retires "ghost entities" (assets that no longer exist but keep firing alerts) and tracks inventory, firmware, and end-of-life exposure.
  • "Deterministic" design: predictable, auditable outcomes in environments where downtime and security are at stake.

The model shift

"IT operations have long been measured by how quickly teams can respond. But as environments become more complex, distributed, and noisy, reaction time alone is no longer enough," said Cullen Childress, SolarWinds' interim CEO and chief product officer. The line summarizes the industry's move: from managing alerts to orchestrating outcomes. Agents take on first-line work; the human team becomes more strategic, designing guardrails, policies, and goal hierarchies.

The template your business can copy

  1. Define the goal before the agent: not "an agent for IT," but "zero repeat incidents" or "cloud spend under budget." The agent pursues the goal, not the loose task.
  2. Guardrails from day one: what it can do alone, what needs approval, what it never touches. The auditable model is the one that survives the first mistake.
  3. Measure outcomes, not activity: Avior tracks progress toward the goal. In your business: time saved, errors avoided, cost reduced — not "how many messages the bot processed."

Frequently asked questions

What is SolarWinds Avior?

A goal-oriented agentic AI system for enterprise IT, unveiled on October 14, 2026. It coordinates specialized agents to diagnose issues, analyze root causes, and advance measurable goals across hybrid infrastructure.

What does "deterministic" mean here?

That the system is designed for predictable, auditable outcomes — critical in environments where variability can translate into downtime or audit exposure.

Does Avior replace the IT team?

Per SolarWinds' framing, it takes on first-line work (diagnosis, triage, goal tracking) so the team becomes more strategic: designing guardrails, policies, and goal hierarchies.

How do I access Avior?

Available starting October 14, 2026 through a guided onboarding program, introduced during SolarWinds Day (October 14–15).
