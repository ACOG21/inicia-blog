Meta put Muse on your wrist: the AI agent reaches Apple Watch and Wear OS

By inicIA · November 11, 2026 · 6 min read

Direct answer

On November 9, 2026, Meta brought its Muse AI agent to Apple Watch and Wear OS: you can now talk to the assistant by voice call, text message or dictation directly from the watch, without taking out your phone. On Apple Watch, the companion app arrives with Muse for iOS version 15.0 and lets you add a Muse complication to the watch face. The iPhone app requires iOS 18 or later and the watch watchOS 9 or later. Meta launched Muse on the App Store in September and added a dedicated iPad app in October: the watch is the third device in two months.

What Muse does on the watch

  • Three ways to talk to it: voice call, text message or dictation, according to TechCrunch. The watch app puts Muse's character, the yeti Jolly, front and center, with quick access from the watch face.
  • The assistant that connects accounts: Muse can connect to personal accounts and carry out tasks, according to the launch coverage. From the wrist, a voice request can start work that used to require opening the phone.
  • Wear OS too, according to Meta: the official Muse account on X announced the same day that Muse reaches Apple Watch and Wear OS. The list of supported watches and the minimum Wear OS version have not been published, and a Google Play search on November 10 did not yet show the Muse app for Android watches. Treat the Wear OS part as announced by Meta, with details pending.
  • Distribution is the news: Muse passed 10 million downloads in the United States within its first 40 days on the App Store, according to TechCrunch, one of only 12 mobile apps to reach that mark in that period. Meta is putting Muse on every screen where its audience already lives.

The warning that comes with the watch

  • An assistant on the wrist sees more: AppleInsider reported in September 2026 that Muse synced 187,000 lines from a user's Apple Messages database on macOS even though Full Disk Access was off. It is an incident reported by AppleInsider in September 2026, and it sets the question every business must ask.
  • Permissions are reviewed per device: each new screen is a new access surface. Before connecting an agent to your accounts on the watch, the phone or the computer, review what data it can read and what actions it can run on each one.
  • Voice is the fastest channel and the riskiest: dictating an instruction from the watch is convenient, and it is also the channel where a misheard word becomes an action. For tasks that move money or customer data, confirmation on screen is still mandatory.
  • Meta has not published the price of its own device: the Muse Charm, a dedicated Tamagotchi-like device shown at the Connect 2026 event in September, has a holiday launch window with no price or final specifications published. The watch is today's access route; the dedicated device is tomorrow's bet.

What to do with this news if you sell with AI

  • Your customer will talk to you from the wrist: if your agent serves customers on WhatsApp or by voice, get ready for short, dictated requests with on-the-go context. Answers must be short and carry a single clear action.
  • Demand permissions per task, not per account: an agent that can read your messages and move money with the same credential is a risk. Separate reading from action and require human approval for the sensitive parts.
  • The race is for the wrist, not for the model: Meta, OpenAI, Google and Anthropic compete to be on every screen. The model that wins is the one a gesture away when the customer needs it.
  • Test the channel before you sell it: install the app, dictate three real requests from your business and measure what it answers and what it executes. An agent that promises in the announcement and fails on the wrist is not a sales channel.

Note: this article summarizes Meta's November 9, 2026 announcement and coverage in TechCrunch, MacRumors, MacObserver and Skimwire. Version requirements are those on the App Store listing for Muse 15.0. The Wear OS part is what Meta announced on X; compatibility details have not been published. The Messages sync incident is the one reported by AppleInsider in September 2026.
