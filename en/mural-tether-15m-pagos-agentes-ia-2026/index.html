Tether Led $15M into Mural: the Infrastructure for AI Agents to Move Money Is Here | inicIA

By inicIA · October 23, 2026 · 6 min read

Direct answer

Mural raised $15 million in a Series A led by Tether (announced Oct 21, 2026, reported by CoinDesk), with North Island Ventures and existing investors participating, bringing its total capital to $28 million. The New York startup —founded by former Palantir engineers— builds infrastructure so AI agents can move money with enterprise controls: transaction limits, approved counterparties and approvals for large payments. It has already processed $2.1 billion since its API launch in 2025, across 128 countries, and its 2026 volume is nearly 6x 2025's. The signal: "agentic finance" is leaving demo land and getting serious rails.

What Mural Does (and Why It Matters)

  • One account, many rails: traditional dollars, local currencies and stablecoins in a single account, bridging legacy banking and blockchains with licenses and bank relationships included.
  • The policy engine is the product: every agent payment passes through rules —spending limits, approved-counterparty lists, human approval for large amounts. It answers the key question: who authorized this, and who is accountable?
  • Real traction, not promises: customers like Deel (global payroll) and Opera already run on Mural. Stablecoin payments roughly doubled in 2025 to ~$390B (McKinsey/Artemis), and agents already made 138M+ payments via the x402 protocol on Base in 12 months (Chainalysis).
  • Tether's line sums it up: "As agents start moving money, that connection to the banks, payment networks and compliance systems the world still runs on becomes essential" (Paolo Ardoino, CEO of Tether).

What to Demand Before Letting an Agent Pay for You

  1. Limits and approvals by default: no agent should move money without daily caps, pre-approved counterparties and human escalation for large amounts. If your vendor doesn't offer this, it's not production-ready.
  2. Agent identity and traceability: every transaction must record which agent initiated it, under what authorization and which policy. Without that trail, an audit becomes impossible.
  3. Separation of roles: paying is not the same as operating. Demand distinct roles (who pays doesn't trade, who trades doesn't transfer) just like in your human finance team.
  4. Start with low-risk payments: subscriptions, top-ups, approved purchases. Payments to new vendors or large amounts stay human-approved until the system proves discipline.

The Honest Caveat

  • The market is early: Dealroom notes that live agentic transactions have been mostly controlled demonstrations. Mastercard added tools in September and six major banks published shared principles, but banks are still working through agent identity, authorization, fraud and liability.
  • Stablecoin ≠ free money: the operational volatility and tax/accounting treatment of stablecoins vary by jurisdiction. Infrastructure solves the "how", not what your accountant says.

Frequently asked questions

What is Mural and how much did it raise?

Mural is a payments-infrastructure startup founded by former Palantir engineers. It raised $15M in a Series A led by Tether (Oct 21, 2026), bringing its total to $28M.

What is "agentic finance"?

The concept of AI agents initiating and executing transactions on behalf of people and businesses, with built-in authorization controls, limits and compliance.

Is there real volume of agent-made payments yet?

Yes, and growing: Mural processed $2.1B since 2025 across 128 countries, and Chainalysis counted 138M+ agent payments via the x402 protocol on the Base network in the 12 months through July.

What controls should I demand before giving an agent payment access?

Spending limits, approved counterparties, human approval for large amounts, an auditable record of every transaction, and separation of roles between who pays and who operates.
