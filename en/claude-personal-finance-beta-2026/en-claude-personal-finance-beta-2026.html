Claude can now read your bank accounts: what Personal Finance is and where the limit sits

By inicIA · November 7, 2026 · 6 min read

Direct answer

On November 5, 2026, Anthropic launched Personal Finance in Claude in beta in the United States: a hub inside Claude where Pro, Max and Ultra subscribers connect bank, credit card, loan and investment accounts and ask Claude about their own money in everyday language. The connection runs through Plaid and is read-only: Claude reads balances and transactions, and cannot move, manage or invest the money. Anthropic says chats inside the finance hub are not used to train its models, and that Claude never asks for or sees the bank password. The rollout is gradual: not every eligible subscriber sees it on day one.

The numbers that matter

  • Pro, Max and Ultra, US only: the beta is for paid individual subscribers in the United States. Pro costs $20 a month (or $17 a month billed yearly), Max $100 and Ultra $200. Free, Team and Enterprise are left out.
  • Up to 16 banks: each user can connect up to 16 banks. Only US banks can be linked, and the section does not appear outside the United States, even over a VPN.
  • Up to 24 months of transactions: Claude can read balances, credit limits, the last four digits of accounts and up to 24 months of transactions with amount, date, merchant and category.
  • 5 steps in 10 minutes: linking via Plaid must be completed within 10 minutes because the Plaid session expires. Access is revoked by unlinking the bank in Claude or disconnecting Claude in Plaid's portal.
  • No ads or sponsored recommendations: Anthropic says Personal Finance shows no ads or sponsored recommendations, and offers no cards, loans or accounts of its own.

Where the limit sits, per Anthropic itself

  • Read-only, for real: Claude cannot transfer money, pay a bill, cancel a subscription or place a trade. For a first release that touches bank accounts, that restriction is the right design call.
  • Figures can mislead in three ways: Anthropic's Help Center warns that balances can be a day or two old; that an account connected through two different logins can be counted twice; and that if Claude cannot read a linked bank, that bank's spending is left out of the figures, which can make the money left before the next payday look larger than it is.
  • What Claude does not read: for investment, loan and credit-card accounts, Claude reads balances and credit limits. It does not read investment holdings, trades, interest rates or loan terms.
  • Anthropic's example, with demo figures: in its example, a user asks where the month's money went and Claude totals $4,309.37 across connected accounts, with more than half on rent and utilities and $1,024.51 on food and drink; then it shows four upcoming payments and $1,393.22 left before the next payday. Those are the company's demo figures, not results from an independent test.
  • The lesson for your business: when an agent touches your customers' sensitive data, the design that matters is the limits: what it can read, what it can never move, and what happens when data arrives late or incomplete. Anthropic published those limits in the first paragraph, not the fine print.

Frequently asked questions

What is Personal Finance in Claude?

A beta hub inside Claude, launched November 5, 2026 in the United States, that connects bank, card, loan and investment accounts via Plaid and answers questions about your own money in everyday language.

Can Claude move my money?

No. Access is read-only: Claude cannot move, manage or invest money, and Anthropic says it is not designed to give investment advice.

Who can use it?

Individual Pro, Max and Ultra subscribers in the United States, with a gradual rollout. Free, Team and Enterprise are left out, and no date has been announced for other countries.

What data does Claude see from my accounts?

Bank and account names, account types, the last four digits, balances, credit limits and up to 24 months of transactions. It never sees the bank password, nor investment holdings, trades or rates.
