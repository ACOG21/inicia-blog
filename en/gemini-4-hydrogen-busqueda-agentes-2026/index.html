Gemini 4 Hydrogen: the cheap model for coding with agents, straight into Search

By inicIA · November 17, 2026 · 6 min read

Direct answer

Gemini 4 Hydrogen is the fast, cheap model of the Gemini 4 family, launched by Google on November 12, 2026. It costs $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 output, with no launch discount, a context of 1 million tokens and a free tier on the API. Google made it the default model of Search AI Mode for all users: it is the model your customers already use when they search, even if your business does not use Gemini.

What Google launched on November 12

  • The third Gemini 4 model: Hydrogen joins Argon and Carbon. Google describes it as the rapid-execution model for everyday work: coding, debugging, answering questions and running agents that use tools. It is a model adapted from Carbon, with less depth and more speed.
  • Flat pricing and no promotion: $1.50 input and $7.50 output per million tokens across the entire 1-million-token window. Hydrogen costs less than Argon and Carbon ($2/$10 at their launch price) and arrives with no introductory price: today's price is the stable price.
  • The only Gemini 4 model with a free tier: Hydrogen is on the Gemini API free tier and in Google AI Studio, and it reaches free users of the Gemini app through automatic selection. Argon and Carbon require the paid tier.
  • Four thinking levels, with a new xhigh: Hydrogen adds the xhigh thinking-effort level. Google claims it beats Argon on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index with 51 points against 50 at its default high level, and 54 at xhigh. These are Google's figures; independent verification published so far confirms the index score.

What changes in Search (and why it affects you even if you do not use Gemini)

  • Hydrogen is the default model of AI Mode: Google put it in Search AI Mode for all users on launch day, and calls it the biggest model upgrade to Search yet. AI Mode and AI Overviews answers run on Hydrogen for the harder questions.
  • Query fan-out gets wider: with Hydrogen's reasoning, Search runs simultaneous queries across subtopics and links to more sites than before. Google says this surfaces sites the user would not have discovered. Your content now competes against that wider spread.
  • Speed is the product: Google claims Hydrogen completes tasks 2.5 times faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash at its default setting, and more than triples its score on Terminal-Bench 4.1 (57% against 18.2%). Speed decides which model runs in production, because latency is cost.
  • Hydrogen replaces Flash: Google retires the Flash name: Hydrogen is the recommended upgrade from Gemini 3.8 Flash and earlier models. Teams running Flash migrate to Hydrogen without leaving the family.

What to do in your business this week

  • Try Hydrogen free before paying for another model: the free tier of the API and AI Studio lets you run your real tasks on Hydrogen and measure cost and quality before committing budget.
  • Measure cost per task, not per token: Hydrogen costs $1.50/$7.50 per million tokens, but a model that thinks longer can spend more tokens per task. Run the same task on Hydrogen and on your current model and compare the total cost per finished task.
  • Check how Search sees you now: AI Mode runs on Hydrogen and links to more sites. Search for your services the way a customer would and check whether your content appears in the answers and in the fan-out links.
  • If you run agents, evaluate multi-model mode: Google positions Hydrogen as the execution engine next to Carbon and Argon: Hydrogen executes fast, Carbon and Argon reason deep. In Antigravity, Orchestrator switches models inside the same session according to the task.

Note: this article summarizes Google's announcement of November 12, 2026, the Gemini 4 Hydrogen guide, and coverage by The Verge, The Library Estate, Kingy AI and Digital Matters. The benchmark and speed figures are Google's claims; independent verification published so far confirms the Intelligence Index score. Prices are Google's official list prices.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Gemini 4 Hydrogen cost?

$1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 output, with flat pricing across the entire 1-million-token window. It has no launch discount: that is its stable price.

Does Hydrogen have a free tier?

Yes. It is the only Gemini 4 model with a free tier on the Gemini API and in Google AI Studio, and it reaches free users of the Gemini app through automatic selection.

What is the xhigh thinking level?

It is the fourth thinking-effort level Google adds with Hydrogen. Google claims that at xhigh Hydrogen reaches 54 points on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, against 51 at its default high level and 50 for Argon.

Where does Hydrogen run today?

On the Gemini API, Google AI Studio, Gemini for Business, the Gemini app, Google Search for all users and, for Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers, in Google Antigravity and in Gemini in Google Sheets.
