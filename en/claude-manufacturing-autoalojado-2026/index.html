Claude enters the factory and your own server: Manufacturing and self-hosted agents

By inicIA · November 17, 2026 · 6 min read

Direct answer

On November 12, 2026 Anthropic launched two pieces that move Claude from the desktop into company operations: Claude for Manufacturing, a plugin for Team and Enterprise plans with 17 skills and connectors to Autodesk Fusion, Infor, Microsoft 365 and Oracle NetSuite, and the private beta of self-hosted Managed Agents, which lets you run the full agent service inside your own cloud or on your servers, with your credentials and your session history under your control.

Claude for Manufacturing: the agent works on the factory's systems

  • 17 skills for the factory's office work: the plugin includes skills for tasks such as drafting quotes and quality reports. The agent works on documents, data and management systems, not on the plant's machines.
  • Connectors to the systems you already use: Autodesk Fusion for design, Infor and Oracle NetSuite for enterprise management, and Microsoft 365 for documents and email. The agent reads and writes where the operation's information already lives.
  • For Team and Enterprise plans: the launch reaches Claude's team and enterprise plans, not the individual plan. It is an enterprise sales piece, with the control and administration those plans require.
  • The pattern is verticalization: after Astra for Law and the payroll and finance agents with Workday, Anthropic keeps specializing Claude by industry. The question for your sector is when its plugin arrives, not whether it arrives.

Self-hosted agents: the whole service lives inside your perimeter

  • What runs on your infrastructure: the agent server, its PostgreSQL 16 or higher database, and the workers where agents run their tools. Session history lives in your database, and credentials are kept outside the sandbox where the agent's code runs.
  • You choose where model calls go: by default to the Claude API, or to Amazon Bedrock, Google Cloud, Microsoft Foundry or your own gateway. The model still runs outside your network, but you decide the route.
  • Access is through a private beta: companies request access through their Anthropic account team. Since May, sandboxes could already be self-hosted; now the rest of the service can be self-hosted too.
  • Some features are missing for now: the self-hosted version lacks webhooks, scheduled runs, the Files API and MCP OAuth credentials, according to launch coverage. And nothing migrates from the hosted service: agents and saved data have to be rebuilt.

What to do in your business this week

  • If security was holding your agents back, revisit the argument: self-hosting removes the objection that credentials and history live outside. Ask your security team to evaluate the private beta against its real requirements.
  • Count the cost of operating, not just licensing: self-hosting means you patch the server, back up the database and configure the firewall. Without a database, a restart erases agents and sessions. New control brings new operations.
  • If you are in manufacturing, ask for the list of the 17 skills: compare each skill with a real process in your operation and measure how many office-work hours it covers before signing.
  • Decide where data lives before deploying agents: with self-hosting you decide the model provider and the gateway. That decision is governance, not engineering: put it in writing before the first agent in production.

Note: this article summarizes Anthropic's launch of November 12, 2026 and coverage by Help Net Security, The Next Web, AI Weekly and the Claude launch record. The features missing from the self-hosted version and the deployment details come from Anthropic's documentation and launch coverage; no pricing was published.

Frequently asked questions

What is Claude for Manufacturing?

It is a Claude plugin for Team and Enterprise plans, launched November 12, 2026, with 17 skills for the factory's office work (quotes, quality reports) and connectors to Autodesk Fusion, Infor, Microsoft 365 and Oracle NetSuite.

What does it mean that Managed Agents are self-hosted?

That the company runs the agent server, its database and the workers in its own cloud or on its servers, with credentials and session history inside its own infrastructure, instead of Anthropic's.

Does the Claude model also run inside my company?

The model still runs outside your network. What you choose is where model calls go: to the Claude API, Amazon Bedrock, Google Cloud, Microsoft Foundry or your own gateway.

How do I access the self-hosted Managed Agents private beta?

Through Anthropic's account team. The private beta opened November 12, 2026, and no pricing was published and no customers were named.
