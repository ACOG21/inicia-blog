Digital twin: what it is and how it serves your business

By inicIA · September 26, 2026 · 8 min read

Direct answer

A digital twin is a virtual replica that behaves like its real-world counterpart. The classic definition comes from industry: a living copy of an object or a process that stays updated with real data to monitor, simulate and optimize how it works.

The version that is transforming businesses is the human one: an AI avatar with your face, your voice and your knowledge, trained on your company's information. It talks to your customers, answers with your judgment, sells, books appointments and serves customers 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. It is your digital self working while you sleep, record content or meet with a client.

What a digital twin is: the definition that comes from industry

IBM defines it precisely: a digital twin is a virtual representation of a physical object or system that uses real-time data to faithfully reflect its behavior, performance and conditions.

The concept was born in manufacturing and engineering. A digital twin of a turbine reveals how it is running right now and projects how it will run in the future. Companies apply the same logic across factories, buildings, aircraft and supply chains: monitoring operations, testing scenarios, predicting failures and making data-driven decisions.

The big names behind this technology include Siemens, General Electric, Nvidia, IBM, Bentley and Microsoft. It is one of the strongest bets of Industry 4.0, and its logic is simple: if you can see everything in a virtual replica, you can optimize everything in the real world.

The twist: when the digital twin is you

With generative AI, the concept took an unexpected leap: the replica is no longer limited to machines. The "object" being replicated can now be a person.

A human digital twin is an AI avatar that combines three elements. First, your face and your voice: it is trained on your photos, videos and recordings until it recreates you realistically. Second, your knowledge: it is fed with your business information (your services, your prices, your frequently asked questions, your answers to objections, the way you talk). Third, the ability to converse and act in real time: it serves a customer, qualifies a lead, books an appointment or follows up on WhatsApp.

The result is a replica of you that works with your judgment when you cannot be there. The difference from a recorded video is total: the digital twin does not repeat a script, it answers each person in their own conversation, adapting every response to the context.

Three real examples that already exist

H&M. In March 2025, the brand announced the creation of digital twins of 30 real-life models to use them in advertising and social media content. The project was developed with the Swedish firm Uncut, and in July 2025 H&M launched its first campaign with these digital doubles. The detail that makes the model fair: each model keeps the rights to their digital twin and is paid for every use.

Nike and Serena Williams. In 2022, Nike and AKQA created "Never Done Evolving": an AI-generated match between 17-year-old Serena Williams (her first Grand Slam, the 1999 US Open) and 35-year-old Serena (her 23rd title, the 2017 Australian Open). Machine learning modeled each era's playing style (decision making, shot selection, reactivity) from archival footage. The piece won the Grand Prix in Digital Craft at Cannes Lions 2023.

DeepBrain AI and Shinhan Bank. DeepBrain AI launched AI bank tellers in Shinhan Bank branches in South Korea: interactive AI versions modeled on five real employees, able to handle 64 consultation tasks (deposits, loan applications and more) on digital desks and smart kiosks, while keeping a human touch.

What a digital twin needs to work well

The twin's quality depends on what you feed it for training. These five elements decide whether the result comes out convincing or mediocre:

  • Real material of you: photos, videos and voice recordings. The more varied the material, the more natural the result.
  • Your knowledge in writing: frequently asked questions, processes, typical objections and decision criteria. This is what makes it sound like you and not like a generic robot.
  • Clear operating rules: what it can resolve on its own and when it hands the case to a human.
  • Control and consent: your face, your data and your approval for every use. A serious digital twin always works under your rules.
  • Measurement: how many conversations it resolved, how many leads it qualified, how many appointments it booked. What gets measured gets better.

Frequently asked questions

Is a digital twin the same as a chatbot?

They are in the same family, but the digital twin plays at a higher level: it has your face, your voice and your knowledge trained on your company's data. A chatbot answers general questions; a digital twin talks like you, with your judgment, and can also qualify leads, book appointments and follow up. It is the difference between an answering machine and a digital version of you.

Can any business have a digital twin?

Yes. It works especially well in businesses where the owner or lead salesperson is the face of the business: realtors, consultants, coaches, clinics, agencies. If your business depends on you to answer and sell, a digital twin multiplies your capacity without hiring more people. To understand the foundation of all this, read what artificial intelligence is and how companies use it.

What does it take to create a digital twin?

Three things: your own material (photos, videos, voice recordings), your business knowledge (services, prices, frequently asked questions, answers to objections) and the rules for how you want it to treat your customers. With that, your avatar is trained and connected to your channels: web, WhatsApp, social media.

Does a digital twin replace my team?

It multiplies it. The twin handles the repetitive and urgent around the clock: questions, lead qualification, scheduling and follow-up. Your team focuses on what needs human judgment and real presence: closing deals, complex cases and relationships.
