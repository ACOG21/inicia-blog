ChatGPT now has a business front door: Business Agents arrive in beta for US companies

By inicIA · November 12, 2026 · 6 min read

Direct answer

On November 10, 2026, OpenAI launched Businesses in ChatGPT in beta: companies can claim and verify their profile in ChatGPT, keep their information and product catalogs current, and see aggregated discovery metrics, such as mentions and clicks to their website. US e-commerce and online-service businesses can also create a Business Agent: an agent that serves interested customers in clearly labeled conversations, separate from the assistant's main response. The rollout is a beta in the United States. OpenAI says individual conversations are not shared with businesses and that sponsored placements are labeled.

What your business can do inside ChatGPT

  • Claim and verify the profile: through Business Hub, the company confirms it is who it says it is and updates its information. Verification is the step that separates a listing you control from a listing ChatGPT assembled from scattered web data.
  • Keep the catalog current: the company maintains its information and product feeds. When a customer asks about a product, the answer comes from what the company published, with the prices and availability the company maintains.
  • See how customers discover you: Business Hub shows aggregated discovery metrics: how often ChatGPT mentions the company and how many clicks reach its website. These are aggregate discovery figures.
  • Serve customers with a Business Agent: eligible e-commerce and online-service businesses can offer an agent that answers questions from interested customers in labeled conversations. The customer always sees they are talking to the company's agent, in a labeled conversation.

Why this changes your business storefront

  • ChatGPT becomes a discovery channel: until now, a customer reached your business through Google, Instagram or a referral. Now they can arrive by asking ChatGPT what to buy and where. The company with a claimed, current listing shows up with its own data.
  • Outdated information costs sales: if your ChatGPT listing shows an old price, an old schedule or a product you no longer sell, the customer gets that answer and leaves. Business Hub is the tool that prevents that.
  • The agent serves at the moment of decision: the Business Agent steps in when the customer is already interested and has questions. It is the equivalent of having a salesperson in the store at the exact moment the customer looks at the product.
  • The beta is for US businesses: access is limited for now, and only for e-commerce and online services. The company that qualifies and enters early learns the channel before its competitors do.

What to do in your business this week

  • Check how ChatGPT describes you today: ask about your company, your products and your prices. Write down everything that is outdated or wrong. That is your correction list for Business Hub.
  • Claim your profile as soon as you can: verification is the first step. A verified listing with your own data is the only one you control.
  • Prepare your agent's answers: if you qualify for a Business Agent, define which questions it answers, what it offers and when it hands the customer to a human. An agent without a script answers anything; an agent with a script sells.
  • Measure the channel like any other: the mentions and clicks in Business Hub are your metrics. If ChatGPT brings you customers, the channel deserves budget and maintenance, just like your website.

Note: this article summarizes OpenAI's announcement of November 10, 2026 and coverage in The Verge, TechCrunch and Reuters. The program is in beta in the United States for e-commerce and online-service businesses. Eligibility details, pricing and expansion dates have not been published.
