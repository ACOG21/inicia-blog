Anthropic enters its key week: the roadshow can start November 10 and the listing points to the week of the 16th

By inicIA · November 6, 2026 · 6 min read

Direct answer

Anthropic reaches its key week. The company filed its public prospectus (Form S-1) on October 26 and the amendment with third-quarter results on November 2. The SEC rule requiring 15 days between the prospectus publication and the roadshow sets November 10 as the earliest date to start presenting the offering to investors. Reports point to a Nasdaq debut under the symbol ANT in the week of November 16. The valuation under discussion runs from $1.5 to $2 trillion, against a last confirmed private valuation of $965 billion in the May 2026 Series H round. The offering price and share count remain blank in the filing: the amendment that sets them is what is still missing.

The numbers that matter

  • November 10, earliest roadshow date: the SEC's 15-day rule runs from the S-1 publication on October 26. Reports expect the roadshow in the week of November 9.
  • Week of November 16, reported debut: The Wall Street Journal reported that Anthropic aims to list that week. On November 5, Polymarket put a 63% probability on a listing before November 20 and about 84% before November 30.
  • $1.5 to $2 trillion under discussion: Bloomberg reported on October 28 investor indications of $1.4 to $1.5 trillion; the $2 trillion high end comes from August reports. At $1.5 trillion, the company would cost about 43 times its trailing twelve-month revenue ($35.28 billion).
  • $965 billion confirmed in May: the last confirmed private valuation, from the Series H. The range discussed for the listing would already be a step up from that mark.
  • 22 underwriters, led by Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley: the prospectus names them. The market expects a raise of up to $100 billion per reports, a figure the filing still does not confirm.

What to watch this week if your business uses Claude

  • The Bitget subscription is a third-party product, not the listing: the Bitget exchange opened a subscription to the listing on November 5 through its IPO Prime product, with a VIP round through November 9 ($1 billion total cap, $3 million individual cap, $100 minimum, 12% upfront fee) and a public round from November 10 ($200 million cap, $25,000 individual limit, 5% fee). It is the exchange's product, with its own fees and historical allocation rates of 40% to 80%; it is not Anthropic's offering.
  • The amendment with the price is the missing document: when Anthropic files the amendment with a price range and share count, the calendar becomes concrete. Until then, date and valuation are reports.
  • Your vendor becomes a public company: if the listing happens, Anthropic's pressure for revenue and margins becomes public and quarterly. That affects the future price of Claude and the stability of your cost per task, the same way it already works with the other listed vendors.
  • Growth is cooling in percentage terms: third-quarter revenue ($16.956 billion) grew 47% against the prior quarter, against 144% between the first and second quarters. The market is already pricing that into the valuation under discussion.

Note: this article summarizes Anthropic's November 2, 2026 amendment to Form S-1 and November 5 coverage from RoboForex, MEXC, NAI500 and The Code W. The roadshow date, listing date, valuation and raise size are reported, not confirmed by the company. Polymarket data measures what traders expect, not what the company has set. This is not investment advice. Third-quarter results are detailed in the November 5 article on the amendment.
