10 questions to ask before hiring AI

By inicIA · October 2, 2026 · 7 min read

Direct answer

Before signing, require written answers on data, security, model ownership, total costs, success metrics, and exit plan. Start with a measurable 30-day pilot, a named owner, and a test with real business data.

What to review

Document the current process, clean the input data, and define who approves each system output. Test in a scoped area, measure against a baseline, and scale only what proves return.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I start?

With a scoped pilot, a measurable goal, and a named owner.

When do results show?

Compare at 30, 60, and 90 days against the day-one baseline.

Do I need an in-house tech team?

You can rely on an implementation partner; keep an internal process owner.
