Sema4 3.0 promises 80% fewer tokens with a forensic audit of every agent step

By inicIA · November 2, 2026 · 6 min read

Direct answer

Sema4.ai unveiled version 3.0 of its enterprise AI agent platform in late October 2026, built on one central idea: moving an agent's repetitive work out of the model and into code reviewed and approved by the customer. The company reports that shift cuts token consumption by up to 80% on repeated data queries and delivers results 4x faster than generating the query with the model every time. The same release adds a forensic log documenting every agent step with its before and after.

The three pieces of the launch

  • Verified Queries: once an agent solves how to access a dataset well, that pattern is approved and reused. Sema4.ai reports 80% fewer tokens and 4x faster results than generating the query with the model each time.
  • Verified Logic: the same principle applied to repeated calculations, checks and transformations. The company reports 50% fewer tokens and 30% faster runs on that repeated work.
  • Enhanced DataFrames: in-memory calculations across large structured datasets, without sending every row through the model's context window.
  • Shared workspaces: teams sharing agents, skills, memories, data and files under shared governance, instead of agents isolated per person.
  • Sai assistant: an integrated assistant that suggests improvements to workflows, instructions, skills and data context based on real usage.

Governance as a product feature

  • Forensic audit per step: activity logs with before-and-after changes, an agent timeline, search and filters, to show an auditor what the agent did and why.
  • Certifications cited: the platform cites ISO 27001, SOC 2, HIPAA and GDPR in its security and lifecycle management framework, with a control-room dashboard for enterprise management.
  • Zero-copy access, on the way: the company says zero-copy data access is planned for future releases, to reduce unnecessary data duplication during agent work.
  • The missing figure: Sema4.ai did not disclose pricing in the announcement. The platform is offered as an enterprise subscription, aimed at large enterprises and software vendors scaling agents beyond pilot.
  • The evaluation question: the 80% saving applies to repeated work with approved patterns. A business should measure how much of its real work is repeated before projecting that saving onto its total bill.

Note: this article summarizes Sema4.ai's announcement and CityBiz, The AI Journal and WebWire coverage from late October 2026. Token, speed and certification figures are company-reported, with no independent verification published as of launch.
