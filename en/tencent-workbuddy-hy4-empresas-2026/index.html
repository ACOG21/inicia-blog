Tencent takes WorkBuddy to enterprises outside China and previews Hy4: the office agent that already handled 9 billion tasks

By inicIA · October 31, 2026 · 6 min read

Direct answer

At the 2026 Global Digital Ecosystem Summit, held in Shenzhen on October 29 and 30, 2026, Tencent presented the enterprise capabilities of its office productivity agent, WorkBuddy, and previewed the coming general release of its Hy4 and Hy4 Flash models. The date caveat matters: Hy4 was previewed, not generally released. What is already in production is WorkBuddy, which, per Tencent, has handled more than 9 billion task requests since its March 2026 launch, with adoption across more than 50 industries and 50% of China's top 500 enterprises.

What WorkBuddy does in the enterprise

  • It connects with the tools the team already uses: WorkBuddy connects with messaging applications and enterprise systems, to complete tasks inside the usual tools, without forcing the team to switch environments for each request.
  • Model per task: customers can choose the model that best fits each task, with security controls designed for enterprise use, per Tencent.
  • International expansion: Tencent is taking WorkBuddy to international enterprises, alongside CodeBuddy, Miora, its Agent Development Platform, MAGIC AI and Agent Runtime, so Chinese and international teams can collaborate on the same AI workbench.
  • Cross-application coordination in China: in China, Tencent also introduced Buddy One, a cross-application collaboration system that lets WorkBuddy coordinate tasks across its office applications and specialized business agents.

Hy4 and Hy4 Flash: previewed, not available

  • Hy4: per Tencent, general release is coming soon, with stronger performance on long-horizon and real-world productivity tasks, higher reasoning efficiency and visual understanding for the first time.
  • Hy4 Flash: a more cost-effective version that carries over Hy4's visual understanding and agentic capabilities.
  • TokenHub: through Tencent Cloud TokenHub, enterprises can access Hy models alongside third-party models, with a single API and usage, governance and billing management in one interface.
  • The international base Tencent cites: the company says its overseas customer base doubled over the past year, with triple-digit growth in demand for its AI products and services. Those are company claims in its release, not audited figures.
  • The reading for your business: the WorkBuddy case shows the pattern repeating in 2026: the office agent wins when it lives inside the team's tools and when the customer can switch models per task. The risk is buying a model preview as if it were an available product: Hy4 is still a preview, and its real performance will be measured when general release arrives.
