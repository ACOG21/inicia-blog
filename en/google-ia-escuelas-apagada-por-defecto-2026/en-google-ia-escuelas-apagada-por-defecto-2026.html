Google switches AI off by default in schools: what changes with Guided Learning and what it means for your business

By inicIA · November 6, 2026 · 6 min read

Direct answer

On November 3, 2026, Google announced a fundamental change in AI for education: Gemini, Gemini Notebook, Gemini in Classroom and Google Search stay off by default in K-12 student accounts, until each school's administrator explicitly turns them on. At the same time, the company launched Guided Learning in Google Classroom worldwide, the Gemini mode that coaches the student step by step instead of handing over the answer, and announced a Guided Learning pilot in Search for student accounts in selected US schools later in November. Google's message is twofold: AI in school is used with teacher guidance, and the default value is off.

The numbers that matter

  • 4 products off by default: Gemini, Gemini Notebook, Gemini in Classroom and Google Search stay disabled in K-12 accounts until the school turns them on. Google says it will share the details with schools in the coming months, before the change takes effect.
  • Guided Learning, global from November 3: the mode is now available in Google Classroom worldwide, as a teacher-led tool anchored in class material, with open-ended questions, step-by-step breakdowns, practice quizzes and visual aids.
  • Search pilot late in November: in selected US schools, Search will start guiding students through certain homework tasks, before any decision to expand to other districts.
  • 5 new countries for teacher training: the Google AI Educator Series expands to Spain, Brazil, Mexico, Australia and sub-Saharan Africa, with new November modules in the US from November 4.
  • Two options in 2027, per reports: 9to5Google, citing Google's comments to The New York Times, reports that in early 2027 schools will have two options: one with AI Overviews and other AI features on, and one with Guided Learning in their place. Google has not confirmed those options in its own posts.

What it means for your business

  • The default value is already a product decision: Google moved from switching AI on for everyone to switching it off until someone asks for it. If you sell software or training to schools, the adoption flow changes: your buyer is now the administrator who decides to turn it on, not the student who already had it.
  • Coaching instead of answering is the growing pattern: Guided Learning sets the pattern institutions accept: AI as a tutor that asks questions and checks steps, not as a shortcut that hands over the answer. The same pattern applies to training inside your company.
  • Deep integration has a cost: per the 9to5Google report, Search and AI are so deeply integrated that a school that keeps AI off loses access to Search entirely. Before switching off an AI feature in your operation, check what else switches off with it.
  • Teacher training is an open market: Google is expanding its free training series to five new countries. If your business trains teams or sells to educational institutions, the AI literacy standard is being set by the big platforms, and your offer competes against free platform training.

Note: this article summarizes Google's November 3, 2026 announcement (Chris Phillips, vice president and general manager of education) and coverage from 9to5Google, The New York Times via CBC, Digit and AI Frontier Post. The early-2027 options and the loss of Search access when AI stays off are reported, not confirmed by Google in its own posts. Details of the controls change will be shared with schools in the coming months.

Frequently asked questions

What did Google announce on November 3, 2026?

That Gemini, Gemini Notebook, Gemini in Classroom and Google Search stay off by default in K-12 student accounts until the school turns them on, and that Guided Learning arrives in Google Classroom worldwide.

What is Guided Learning?

The Gemini mode that coaches the student through a problem with open-ended questions, step-by-step breakdowns, quizzes and visual aids, instead of handing over the answer directly.

When does Guided Learning reach Search?

As a pilot in selected US schools late in November 2026, for student accounts, before any decision to expand.

Why does the default value matter?

Because it defines who adopts AI: with the change, the decision moves from the student to the school administrator, and a school that does not turn AI on also loses Search access, per the reports.
