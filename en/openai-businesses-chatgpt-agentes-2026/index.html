Your business now lives inside ChatGPT: verified profiles and agents that serve customers

By inicIA · November 16, 2026 · 6 min read

Direct answer

OpenAI launched Businesses in ChatGPT on November 10, 2026, in beta: a Business Hub where companies claim their profile, correct the information ChatGPT shows about them and see how often the chatbot mentions them, and a Business Agent that serves customers inside ChatGPT. OpenAI presents the launch as a way to connect with the 1.2 billion people who use ChatGPT each week, according to the company's figure. The verified profile carries a blue check after an identity check. Agents, at launch, are only for ecommerce and online-service businesses in the United States with a website.

The three parts of the launch

  • Business Hub, the control panel: from the ChatGPT sidebar or chatgpt.com/business-hub, the company searches for its business by name, claims the profile and manages its information: website, description, category, logo, delivery and return policies, and a product catalog with prices and availability.
  • Verification in two steps: first the claim is verified with a code by phone or business email, a DNS TXT record on the domain, or the Shopify store. Then identity verification is completed with Persona, which may ask for a government-issued ID, business registration details and proof of representation. The blue check appears only after Persona approval.
  • Insights, the data panel: the Hub shows mentions, profile views, website clicks and discovery topics over the last 28 days. The company never sees individual conversations or user messages. Figures appear only once activity reaches aggregation thresholds.
  • Business Agent, the brand's agent: an eligible company publishes an agent that answers questions about products and policies in a separate, labeled chat, inside ChatGPT. OpenAI builds the agent from the instructions and catalog the company supplies, and keeps control of the agent.

The limits that matter

  • Claiming does not guarantee visibility: OpenAI says claiming a profile does not guarantee higher placement or more sales, and that ChatGPT keeps making independent recommendations. Verification gives control over the facts ChatGPT shows, and the blue check gives trust, but ranking inside the chat stays in OpenAI's hands.
  • Agents do not close purchases yet: according to launch coverage, Business Agents answer questions and guide the customer, but they do not complete purchases or bookings. The purchase still happens on the company's website.
  • Claiming is free at launch: according to VentureBeat, citing Digiday, claiming a profile and publishing a Business Agent are free at launch. OpenAI's VP of Monetization, Benji Shomair, left open the possibility of charging in the future. The company also sells the same agent as a sponsored destination for ChatGPT Ads.
  • Local shops stay out for now: at launch only businesses with a website can claim. Local shops and restaurants can only claim an existing US listing. OpenAI has not said which languages the program supports or a timeline for expansion.

What to do in your business this week

  • Claim your profile today: search for your business in the Business Hub and claim the listing before someone else claims it for you. Claiming is free at launch and gives you control of the facts ChatGPT shows about your company.
  • Correct the facts ChatGPT shows: review the website, description, category and policies. Correct facts are the base of the verified profile and of the agent built on top of it.
  • Read your mentions before investing in an agent: the Insights panel shows how often ChatGPT mentions your business and on which topics. If ChatGPT already recommends you, the agent has an audience; if it never mentions you, the prior work is SEO and reputation, not the agent.
  • Prepare the catalog for the agent: the agent answers with the catalog and instructions you supply. A catalog with updated prices and availability is the difference between a useful agent and an agent that invents answers.

Note: this article summarizes OpenAI's announcement of November 10, 2026, OpenAI's setup guide, and coverage by TechCrunch, VentureBeat, Reuters, Digiday, The Decoder, Unite.AI and i10x. The 1.2 billion weekly users figure is OpenAI's. The program is a beta and its limits change with each expansion.

Frequently asked questions

What is Businesses in ChatGPT?

It is OpenAI's program, launched in beta on November 10, 2026, for companies to claim and manage how they appear in ChatGPT. It has three parts: Business Hub to claim the profile, Insights to see mentions and clicks, and Business Agents to serve customers inside the chat.

Who can publish a Business Agent?

At launch, only eligible ecommerce and online-service businesses in the United States, with a website and a verified profile. Profile claiming is open to eligible businesses in any market where ChatGPT is available.

How much does it cost to claim a profile in ChatGPT?

Claiming a profile and publishing a Business Agent are free at launch, according to VentureBeat citing Digiday. OpenAI left open the possibility of charging in the future.

Does claiming my profile make ChatGPT recommend me more?

OpenAI says claiming does not guarantee higher visibility or more sales. Claiming gives control over the facts ChatGPT shows about your company; recommendations inside the chat stay independent.
