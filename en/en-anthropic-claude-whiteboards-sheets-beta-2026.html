Claude becomes your workspace: Whiteboards and Sheets arrive in beta

By inicIA · November 12, 2026 · 6 min read

Direct answer

On November 10, 2026, Anthropic launched Claude Whiteboards and Claude Sheets in beta, and made Claude Data generally available. Whiteboards is a shared board for thinking with Claude; Sheets is a spreadsheet built and edited with Claude; Claude Data connects the model to the company's data. The same announcement brought interactive connectors: you can open the full version of a tool inside Claude and work in Adobe, Atlassian and Box beside the conversation, editing yourself or asking Claude to edit with you. The tools are on paid plans, and Enterprise administrators have different default settings.

What each new piece does

  • Claude Whiteboards: a shared board where the team and Claude think together. It works for brainstorming, mapping and planning, with the model participating in the board alongside the team.
  • Claude Sheets: a spreadsheet Claude helps build and maintain. The team works with formulas, tables and data inside the same window where it talks to the model.
  • Claude Data: the connection to the company's data, now generally available. It requires a data connector and lets Claude work with real business information, straight from its data sources.
  • Interactive connectors: they open the full version of the tool inside Claude. You work in Adobe, Atlassian or Box next to the conversation, and Claude edits with you in the same document.

Why the chat fell short

  • Real work lives in boards, sheets and documents: a plan is made on a board, a budget is made in a spreadsheet and a report is made in a document. When the model lives only in the chat, the team copies and pastes between tools and the model loses context at every jump.
  • Shared context is the product: in Whiteboards and Sheets, the team and the model look at the same thing. Claude's questions come from the board everyone sees, and the answers stay saved in the work itself, where the team finds them.
  • Competitors are moving the same way: ChatGPT opened its Business Hub the same day and Google is widening Gemini 4 Carbon to developers and enterprises. All three labs are turning the chat into a full workspace, and the one that reaches your team's workflow first keeps your team.
  • Connected data changes the answers: a model with access to your data answers with your figures. Answers with your figures are the ones that serve decisions.

What to do in your business this week

  • Try one real session in Whiteboards: take your team's next planning session and run it on the board with Claude. If the session comes out better than in a shared document, the board stays. If not, you know within an hour.
  • Connect one dataset: pick one report your team checks every week and connect it with Claude Data. One well-connected dataset is worth more than ten half-connected ones.
  • Define who edits what: interactive connectors let Claude edit in your tools. Decide in writing what it can edit on its own and what needs your approval before it touches it.
  • Review the Enterprise permissions: administrators have different default settings. Before opening the tools to the whole team, review who sees what and what data each role can touch.

Note: this article summarizes Anthropic's announcement of November 10, 2026 and coverage in The Verge and industry roundups. Whiteboards and Sheets are in beta; Claude Data is generally available. Per-plan pricing details and broad availability dates have not been published.
