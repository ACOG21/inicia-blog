ChatGPT Enterprise arrives on AWS Marketplace: buying AI through the channel your company already uses

By inicIA · November 10, 2026 · 6 min read

Direct answer

On November 9, 2026, OpenAI listed ChatGPT Enterprise on AWS Marketplace. Organizations can now buy and manage the product through their existing purchasing and billing relationship with Amazon Web Services, and apply that spending to their AWS consumption commitments. Enterprise AI stops being a new purchase from a new vendor and becomes one more line on the cloud bill the company already pays.

What changes in practice

  • The purchase goes through the channel that already exists: the procurement team does not open a new vendor or negotiate a new contract. ChatGPT Enterprise is bought where the company already buys software, with the same terms and the same billing.
  • Spending counts toward the AWS commitment: many companies have AWS consumption commitments worth millions of dollars. Spending on ChatGPT Enterprise applies to that commitment, so the AI is paid for with budget that was already committed.
  • Management stays in one place: buying, managing users and seeing the bill from AWS simplifies administration for the IT team, which already lives in that console.
  • The signal for the market: OpenAI sells through its cloud competitor's channel. The company that dominates models aims to be inside the cloud you already chose.

What your company should do

  • Check your AWS commitment before signing direct: if your company has an AWS consumption commitment, buying through Marketplace can be cheaper in practice than a direct contract with OpenAI, because the spending was already committed.
  • Compare total price: the list price may be the same, but the real cost changes with discounts, commitments and billing. Ask for quotes through both channels and compare.
  • Easy buying does not replace governance: easy to buy does not mean free to use. Define who uses ChatGPT Enterprise, with what data and with what controls, before opening access to the whole company.
  • If you do not use AWS, this does not affect you: the announcement is for companies in Amazon's ecosystem. If your cloud is Google or Azure, your buying channel is different, and the lesson is the same: AI is bought through the channel you already use.

Note: this article summarizes OpenAI's November 9, 2026 announcement, with coverage from the November 10 AI news digests. OpenAI published no specific AWS Marketplace prices for the product in the announcement.
