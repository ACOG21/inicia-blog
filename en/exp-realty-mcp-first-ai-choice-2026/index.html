eXp Realty Launched 'MCP First, AI of Choice': Your Favorite AI, Connected to Your Business Data | inicIA

By inicIA · October 10, 2026 · 7 min read

Direct answer

On October 9, 2026, at its eXpcon convention in Salt Lake City, eXp Realty —the core operating subsidiary of AGNT, Inc. (Nasdaq: AGNT)— announced "MCP First, AI of Choice": instead of building its own AI assistant, the company is publishing connections under the open Model Context Protocol (MCP) standard so its agents can use the AI they already use —Claude, ChatGPT or any other— connected directly to their eXp business data. Once an agent authorizes the connection, they can ask their AI about production numbers, leads, transactions or training progress without leaving the chat window. It is the first visible piece of AGNT OS, the ground-up modernization of how eXp runs, targeted for early 2027. The same announcement included a mortgage joint venture with Newrez, a pay-at-close home prep program offering up to $50,000, and a marketplace for agent-to-agent showing coverage.

What MCP is and why it is the news

MCP (Model Context Protocol) is the open standard that lets AI assistants securely connect to outside tools and data. At launch, connections cover: eXp systems (the Hub, My eXp with production and compensation data, eXp University and eXp Divisions); partner platforms like Canva and the CRMs Lofty, BoldTrail and Cloze; and coming soon, Follow Up Boss and Sisu. Because agents work inside the AI they choose, the experience improves every time a frontier AI lab releases a new model, without waiting for the brokerage to update its platform.

What the CEO said

"Every company in this industry is racing to build the best AI assistant," said Leo Pareja, CEO of eXp Realty. "We asked a different question: Why should agents have to change how they work at all? 'MCP First, AI of Choice' means agents keep using the AI they already trust. We're making sure it can hear from eXp."

3 lessons for your business

  1. Connect before you build. Before paying for your own assistant, ask which of your systems you can expose via MCP to the AI your team already uses. eXp bet that the value is in the data, not in one more app.
  2. The open standard is your insurance. If your AI depends on proprietary connections, you marry a vendor. MCP is open: you switch models without rewriting anything.
  3. Adoption is won by whoever does not ask people to change habits. eXp did not ask its agents to switch AIs: it brought the data to the AI they already use. That is how AI gets adopted in a real company.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Model Context Protocol (MCP)?

It is an open standard that lets AI assistants securely connect to outside tools and data —CRMs, internal systems, platforms— instead of living in isolation. eXp uses it so its agents' AI can "hear" the brokerage's data.

Do I have to be an eXp agent to use it?

This initiative is for eXp agents, but the lesson is universal: any business can expose its systems via MCP to the AI its team already uses, instead of building a proprietary assistant from scratch.

Is it safe to connect my business data to an AI?

The connection is authorized by the user and runs on the MCP standard, designed for controlled access. The golden rule still holds: define what each connection can see before turning it on, just as you define permissions for an employee.

Why didn't eXp build its own assistant?

Because they understood the battle is not the app, it is the data. A proprietary assistant competes against ChatGPT and Claude every six months; an open connection improves on its own every time a new model ships.
