Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia CEO: what it means for your AI supplier

By inicIA · November 14, 2026 · 6 min read

Direct answer

On November 12, 2026, Nvidia announced that Jensen Huang resigned as president, chief executive officer and member of the board, effective immediately, after 33 years leading the company he co-founded. Chief financial officer Colette Kress becomes interim CEO and keeps her CFO role, while Harvey C. Jones becomes the first board chair in Nvidia's history. The board is starting a search for a permanent successor among internal and external candidates, with no published timeline. Nvidia is keeping its third-quarter fiscal 2027 results release for Tuesday, November 17, as scheduled.

The facts of the announcement

  • The resignation came on November 11: the board accepted Huang's resignation that day, according to Nvidia's Form 8-K with the SEC, and the public statement went out on November 12. The statement gives no reason.
  • Colette Kress, 59, takes interim command: she has been Nvidia's executive vice president and chief financial officer since September 2013. She keeps both roles for the duration of the interim period.
  • Harvey C. Jones, 73, chairs the board: former CEO and executive chairman of Synopsys, he is Nvidia's first-ever board chair. The board will weigh internal and external candidates for the permanent role.
  • The stock fell about 13% on Thursday: according to CNBC, the drop left Nvidia's market value below $4 trillion. The November 17 results will be the company's first public reading with Kress in charge.

The context the press is reporting

  • TechCrunch reports the resignation came days after Huang acknowledged a relationship with a woman described as an alleged Chinese spy: the company opened an internal investigation into the matter, according to NPR. These are press reports about Huang's personal conduct, and Nvidia's statement gives no reason for the resignation.
  • Motley Fool reports The Times published a second alleged relationship on November 11, with a direct report, Kimberly Powell: the board met that evening and accepted the resignation. Nvidia has stayed silent on the reports.
  • The business reading is continuity: Huang was Nvidia's public face and the architect of its turn toward AI. With Kress, a finance executive with 13 years at the company, the immediate message is operational stability while the board searches for a successor.

What to do in your business this week

  • Review your dependence on a single chip supplier: if your agents run on Nvidia hardware, write down which part of your operation depends on its roadmap. Dependence on one supplier is a continuity risk, just like dependence on a single model.
  • Listen to the November 17 earnings call: Kress will speak for the first time as interim CEO. What she says about demand, inventory and chip pricing matters more than any headline about the succession.
  • Separate the noise from the plan: a founder-CEO succession generates headlines for weeks. Your buying decision rests on availability, price and support, and those three facts are checked with your supplier, your contract and your invoice.
  • Write down your contingency plan: a second compute supplier, an alternative model and a switching budget. The plan written today costs an afternoon; improvising one in a crisis costs a quarter.

Note: this article summarizes Nvidia's statement of November 12, 2026, its Form 8-K with the SEC and coverage by TechCrunch, Motley Fool, NPR and CNBC. The stock-move figures come from CNBC. The reports about Huang's personal life are press allegations; Nvidia has given no reason for the resignation.
