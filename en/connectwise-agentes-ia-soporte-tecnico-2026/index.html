ConnectWise launched AI agents that investigate the ticket before touching the device: human approval stays in the middle

By inicIA · October 30, 2026 · 6 min read

Direct answer

On October 29, 2026, ConnectWise announced its RMM Agent and intake agents for voice, Teams and email, generally available the same day. RMM Agent investigates the device behind the ticket, tests what is wrong and stages a fix from a vetted catalog for a technician to approve, without leaving the ticket in the professional services automation (PSA) platform. The intake agents gather missing details from the customer before the ticket reaches a technician.

How each agent works

  • RMM Agent: runs read-only diagnostics during investigation and requires a technician's explicit approval before changing anything on the device. The investigation, hypothesis and proposed fix stay inside the ticket.
  • Voice Agent: answers support calls, gathers context and creates structured PSA work.
  • Teams Agent: asks focused questions to collect missing information before creating the ticket.
  • Email Agent: spots gaps in emailed requests and asks the customer for the specific context that is missing.

The numbers, with their source label

  • Partner-reported results, not independently verified: RealBytes cut first touch from one hour to five minutes; Fusion Computing reported 86% fewer escalations; Worldband reported 120% revenue growth. These are announcement and partner figures, not guaranteed results for another business.
  • Diagnosis: launch coverage reports a useful-diagnosis rate of 91% across more than 500 live tickets, typically in about a minute, and 178 team hours saved in 30 days at RealBytes. Those are also partner-reported figures, not an independent measurement.
  • The detail that matters: the agent proposes, the technician approves. That order is the whole design: automation investigates, a person decides the change.
  • Next date: ConnectWise plans to share more roadmap details at IT Nation Connect Global, November 4–6, 2026.

Frequently asked questions

What does ConnectWise's RMM Agent do?

It investigates the device behind a ticket, tests possible causes and stages a fix from a vetted catalog for a technician to approve.

Does the agent change the device on its own?

No. Diagnostics are read-only and every device change requires a technician's explicit approval.

What do the intake agents do?

They collect missing information from the customer by voice, Teams or email before the ticket reaches a technician.

Do the reported savings apply to my business?

Not necessarily. They are partner-reported results from the announcement; each operation should measure its own times before and after.
