SAP TechEd 2026: Joule agents now reason with your company's map, not just its data

By inicIA · October 28, 2026 · 6 min read

Direct answer

At SAP TechEd 2026, per coverage published October 27, 2026, SAP expanded the Ontology Manager of SAP Business Data Cloud (BDC) to give its agents an understanding of how applications relate to one another: it defines relationships between company facts so agents reason more accurately and make decisions across the enterprise. The announcement joins the SAP Reltio solution, presented at SAP Connect on October 6, which brings multidomain master data management into BDC, plus the Dremio lakehouse expansion and the SAP Databricks integration. The Enterprise AI Gateway, described as a premium offering, arrives in the first quarter of 2027 with no published price in the announcement materials.

What SAP announced, piece by piece

  • Expanded Ontology Manager: an AI-native authoring experience that defines relationships between business facts, so agents understand company context rather than just querying scattered data.
  • SAP Reltio in BDC: the Reltio acquisition closed in May 2026, per coverage, and the solution announced October 6 unifies customer, supplier and product records from SAP and non-SAP systems into reusable, trusted data products.
  • Lakehouse with Dremio: BDC adds native support for Apache Iceberg and Apache Polaris plus a federated query engine, for unified access to data without moving or copying it. General availability is planned for the first quarter of 2027.
  • SAP Databricks: Databricks' data engineering and AI enter BDC, with an Application Data Foundation and a Joule-to-Databricks-Genie integration to build a single lakehouse as the foundation for Joule Work.
  • Knowledge Graph and Gateway in Q1 2027: customer-specific Knowledge Graph capabilities and the Enterprise AI Gateway are scheduled for the first quarter of 2027. Joule Work and Joule Studio are rolling out in phased general availability, and a free Joule Studio runtime promotion for select customers runs through December 2026.

Why master data is the agents' bottleneck

  • The same customer, dozens of conflicting records: products look different in ERP, commerce and PLM, and suppliers appear duplicated across regions. Every AI initiative that matters hits that wall.
  • The agent inherits the mess: an agent querying dirty master data does not fail because of the model; it fails because of the source. Governing the data is governing the agent.
  • SAP's thesis: the company positions governed business context as its agents' differentiator, with choice across more than 70 AI models, per Futurum's analysis of the quarter. The model count comes from an analyst, not from an SAP announcement.
  • The necessary complement: SAP's existing Knowledge Graph already maps business fields and tables; Reltio and the lakehouse extend that base to master data and to data outside SAP.

What it means for your business

  • Before buying agents, audit your master data: duplicated or conflicting customer, supplier and product records are the first project, not the second. An agent on dirty data automates mistakes.
  • Confirm what is live in your landscape: rollout is phased and availability varies by customer. Do not plan a project on a TechEd announcement without confirming what is active in your landscape.
  • Price the Gateway before building on it: SAP describes it as a premium offering and published no price. Work out the cost before promotional terms change.
  • Test against your rules: the deciding test is not the demo; it is your agent against your data quality, your authorization rules and your exception handling.
