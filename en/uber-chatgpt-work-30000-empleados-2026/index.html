Uber took ChatGPT Work from 1,000 to 30,000 employees: the playbook of an agent rollout that actually scaled

By inicIA · October 27, 2026 · 6 min read

Direct answer

On October 22, 2026, Uber expanded ChatGPT Work, OpenAI's agent inside ChatGPT Enterprise, from a 1,000-person pilot to its full global workforce of about 30,000 employees. It is Uber's first company-wide deployment of an agentic platform for knowledge work. The agent runs with GPT-6.1 Sol as the default model and connects to Google Drive, Gmail, Google Calendar and Slack, to research information, prepare meeting briefs, build presentations, analyze data and complete cross-app workflows. Engineering teams will keep using Codex for code.

What the pilot did differently

  • 80% of pilot users were not engineers: according to Uber's VP of engineering, Sumanth Sukumar, most used the agent to analyze operational data, localize training materials and build interactive dashboards.
  • 30 engineers in two-week pods: Uber paired AI engineers with operational units in sprints called AI pods, to find concrete tasks to automate.
  • No rigid mandates: Uber did not dictate how the platform must be used. The company said it will watch the workflows employees invent and track productivity and usage patterns to decide future investments.
  • Internal library and training: the rollout comes with an internal AI skills library, approved integrations and dedicated employee training.

The pattern a smaller business can copy

  1. Pilot with real users, not only technicians: if only engineers test it, you measure the toy, not the work.
  2. Pair AI know-how with the person who suffers the task: Uber's two-week pod is, at small scale, a pair that finds one repeated task and automates it end to end.
  3. Decide what you will measure before expanding: Uber talks about productivity and usage patterns. Your version can be simpler: hours saved per week on one concrete task, with a review date.
  4. No published cost or productivity figures: neither Uber nor OpenAI disclosed the contract cost or measured results. Do not copy the headline; copy the method.

The honest note

  • Sources: OpenAI's announcement (October 22, 2026) via PYMNTS, Unite.AI and TechCrunch.
  • What is unknown: the rollout's cost, the pilot's productivity results, and how many of the 30,000 employees will use the tool each week.

Frequently asked questions

What is ChatGPT Work?

OpenAI's agent inside ChatGPT Enterprise that takes multi-step tasks and delivers finished work (briefs, presentations, spreadsheets, web apps) instead of only chat replies.

How many Uber employees will use it?

The full global workforce, about 30,000 employees, after a 1,000-person pilot.

Which tools does it connect to?

Google Drive, Gmail, Google Calendar and Slack, per the announcement. Engineering keeps Codex for code.

How much did the rollout cost?

Not disclosed. Neither Uber nor OpenAI published the contract cost or pilot productivity figures.
