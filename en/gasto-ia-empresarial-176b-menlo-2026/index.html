Enterprise AI spending hit $176.8 billion in 2026: agents now spend more than humans

By inicIA · November 5, 2026 · 6 min read

Direct answer

On November 3, 2026, Menlo Ventures published its 2026: The State of Enterprise AI report: enterprise AI spending reached $176.8 billion, 5.4 times the $33 billion of 2025, accelerating from 3.9x growth the year before. The deeper shift: agents are now the biggest consumers of AI models. On OpenRouter, agents generate 80% of all tokens, about 7 times human-driven usage. Since agent traffic overtook human traffic in February, agent token consumption has grown 37x, against 5x for humans. And the money concentrates: Anthropic, OpenAI and Google capture 69% of enterprise AI spending, up from 50% in 2025.

The numbers that matter

  • $176.8 billion, 5.4x more: enterprise AI spending rose from $33 billion in 2025 to $176.8 billion by September 2026, per the report.
  • Anthropic 55%, OpenAI 31%, Google 9%: in enterprise model API spending, Anthropic rose from 40% to 55%, OpenAI from 27% to 31%, and Google fell from 21% to 9% in a year.
  • 80% of tokens come from agents: on OpenRouter, agents generate 4 of every 5 tokens, about 7 times human usage.
  • Open source weighs in usage, not in dollars: open models handle about 1 in 5 tokens, but only about 1 in 25 dollars of model spending.
  • The study's base: a survey of nearly 400 US enterprise decision-makers overseeing more than $4 billion in AI spending, plus a bottom-up market analysis. It is the fourth annual edition of the report.

What changes for your business

  • Budget by task, not by license: if agents consume 80% of tokens, your AI cost depends on how many tasks they run and what each costs, not on how many users you have.
  • Pick the vendor by use case: with Anthropic at 55% of API spending and OpenAI at 31%, the practical question is which model performs best on your concrete tasks, tested on 30 to 50 real tasks before signing.
  • Open source enters through price: 1 in 5 tokens already runs on open models. For routine, cost-sensitive tasks, testing an open model before paying frontier prices is worth it.
  • Concentration raises lock-in risk: three labs capture 69% of spending. A price or terms change by one of them moves your whole cost, so design agents that can switch models without a rewrite.

Note: this article summarizes Menlo Ventures' November 3, 2026 release and coverage of the report. Menlo Ventures is an investor in Anthropic and OpenRouter, two of the companies the report measures, a fact its own release discloses. The spending and share figures come from the report and describe the US enterprise market.
