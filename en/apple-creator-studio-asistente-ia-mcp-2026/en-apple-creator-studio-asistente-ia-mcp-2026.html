Apple opens Keynote, Pages and Numbers to AI agents: beta assistant and MCP in November

By inicIA · October 28, 2026 · 6 min read

Direct answer

On October 27, 2026, Apple announced a Creator Studio update arriving in November as a free update for current subscribers. Keynote, Pages and Numbers will get a beta intelligent assistant, built on Apple models and OpenAI, that understands document context. All three apps will also gain support for the Model Context Protocol (MCP), the open standard that lets outside AI agents read content, make edits and run automated actions inside documents.

What the assistant does in each app

  • Keynote: helps design, animate and refine slides.
  • Pages: drafts, restructures and fine-tunes written content.
  • Numbers: calculates, visualizes and organizes data, recommends chart types with a one-tap preview, and builds custom tables.
  • Keynote also adds the .keyx format: an open JSON-based interchange format designed so outside tools, developer workflows and AI agents can read it.
  • Logic Pro gets a beta assistant too (Mac only), and Pixelmator Pro adds Generate Fill: select part of an image and describe the change in natural language.

The limits to read before celebrating

  • Beta and US English only: every assistant launches in beta and in US English only in this phase.
  • Hardware and system: an Apple Intelligence-capable device running iOS 27, iPadOS 27 or macOS 27 or later is required. MCP support in Keynote, Pages and Numbers is Mac only, on macOS 27 or later.
  • Subscription: the update is free for current subscribers; for new subscribers, Creator Studio costs $12.99 per month or $129 per year in the US, with a one-month free trial and student pricing of $2.99 per month or $29.99 per year.
  • No day inside November: Apple gave no exact launch date. And the fine detail of which part of a document each model processes when the assistant answers is still pending.

What it means for your business

  • Your documents become an agent surface: if an outside agent can read and edit your decks, reports and spreadsheets, your documents stop being files and become systems an agent can operate.
  • Govern access before connecting it: define which documents an agent may touch, with which permissions and with what change log. An agent with unlimited editing access is a new employee without supervision.
  • Start with one workflow, not the whole company: a good first case is a repetitive document, such as a monthly report in Numbers or a proposal template in Pages, with human review before sending.
  • The gap is closed: Keynote, Pages and Numbers had trailed Copilot in Microsoft 365 and Gemini in Google Workspace on built-in AI. Apple closes that gap with OpenAI's help, and MCP may matter more than the assistant itself, because it opens the apps to agents the company already uses.

Frequently asked questions

When does Apple's assistant reach Keynote, Pages and Numbers?

In November 2026, as a free update for current Creator Studio subscribers. Apple announced no exact day.

What do I need to use it?

An Apple Intelligence-capable device on iOS 27, iPadOS 27 or macOS 27 or later, and a Creator Studio subscription. MCP support needs a Mac on macOS 27 or later.

What is MCP and why does it matter?

It is the Model Context Protocol, an open standard that lets an outside AI agent read, edit and run actions in an application. Here, it opens Apple's documents to agents from any company.

Is the assistant available in Spanish?

Not in this phase: the assistants launch in beta and in US English only, per Apple's notes.
