Google launched Universal Cart: your store now competes inside Google's cart

By inicIA · October 26, 2026 · 6 min read

Direct answer

On October 15, 2026, Google launched Universal Cart in the United States in Search (including AI Mode) and the Gemini app: a cart that holds products from several stores at once, alerts you when a price drops or stock returns, surfaces promo codes, and lets you pay with Google Pay at participating merchants or transfer the cart to the merchant's site. Checkout runs on the Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP), Google's open standard for connecting inventory to AI shopping surfaces. Launch partners include Nike, Sephora, Target, Ulta Beauty, Walmart and Wayfair, plus Shopify merchants.

What changed for sellers

  • Discovery moved to Google: the shopper builds a basket comparing stores inside Google's cart, not inside your store. Your product listing, price and return policy compete in that space.
  • The merchant stays the seller: according to Google, the merchant keeps the sale, the order confirmation and customer support. That part did not change with the launch.
  • The feed is the storefront: products reach the cart from Google's Shopping Graph, with more than 60 billion listings. An outdated feed, with unclear pricing or no return policy, is a product AI cannot sell.
  • YouTube stayed out of the launch: although mentioned in May at Google I/O, YouTube and Gmail were not part of the October 15 rollout.

What to do before peak season

  1. Audit your Merchant Center this week: prices, availability, promotions, and return and support policies, clean and current.
  2. Separate measurement from illusion: tag orders that come from Google so you know how much of your sales already depends on that channel.
  3. Set your promotion rule: decide which codes and discounts may appear in the cart and which stay only on your site.
  4. Prepare post-sale support: if the order comes through Google, the customer will still come to you when something fails. Have that flow ready.

The honest note

  • One question stays unanswered: Google did not clarify at launch whether ads will appear inside the cart. Merchant analyses flag it as an open question, not a fact.
  • The rollout is U.S.-only for now: other markets use Merchant Center tools for now, such as AI Mode presence reports.
  • Sources: Google (The Keyword, October 15, 2026) via PYMNTS and Unite.AI, and Agentic Commerce Report analysis (October 18, 2026).

Frequently asked questions

What is Universal Cart?

A Google cart, in Search and the Gemini app, that holds products from several stores, alerts you to price drops and restocks, and lets you pay with Google Pay or move the cart to the merchant's site.

Does Google keep the sale?

According to Google, no: the merchant stays the seller of the transaction, with order confirmation and customer support.

How does my product get into the cart?

Through Google Merchant Center and the Shopping Graph. Checkout inside Google requires the Universal Commerce Protocol.

Is it available outside the U.S.?

The October 15, 2026 launch was in the U.S. Other markets do not have the cart yet.
