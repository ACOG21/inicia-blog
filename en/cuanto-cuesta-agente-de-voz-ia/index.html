How Much Does an AI Voice Agent Cost in 2026? Real Pricing

By inicIA · October 1, 2026 · 7 min read

Direct answer

An AI voice agent costs between $0.08 and $0.15 per minute all-in in 2026 on most platforms (Vapi, Retell, Bland, ElevenLabs). Translated to monthly: 1,000 minutes a month is $80 to $150; 2,000 minutes is $160 to $300. Most small businesses land between $50 and $300 a month. The initial build runs $600 to $3,000 one-time, depending on complexity (2026 market data: gideonwafula.com, reviews.wildrunai.com, awesomeagents.ai).

What the price is made of: three layers

The per-minute price is not a single number: it is three layers that add up.

  • Agent platform: Vapi charges a base of about $0.05 per minute; Retell, $0.07 to $0.10; Bland AI, about $0.09 bundled; ElevenLabs Conversational AI, $0.08 to $0.12 plus the monthly subscription.
  • Voice and model: voice and language-model costs pass through based on usage. On Vapi this brings the typical total to $0.08 to $0.15 per minute, and up to $0.33 in premium setups.
  • Telephony: the phone number and call minutes (Twilio runs about $0.013 to $0.035 per minute). ElevenLabs uses your own Twilio account, which is why its total lands at $0.10 to $0.16 per minute.

When comparing platforms, always ask for the all-in price: base + voice + telephony. A low base price with high pass-through can cost more than a bundled plan.

The build cost: $600 to $3,000

Before paying for the first minute, the agent has to be built: conversation script, integration with your calendar or CRM, testing with real calls, and tone adjustments. The 2026 market puts that work between $600 and $3,000 one-time:

  • Low range ($600-$1,200): a simple flow, like answering calls and booking into your calendar.
  • Mid range ($1,200-$2,000): prospect qualification, CRM integration, and handoff to your team.
  • High range ($2,000-$3,000): multiple flows, multiple languages, or custom integrations with your systems.

How to calculate your number in 2 minutes

Multiply your estimated minutes by the all-in price. Illustrative example: 50 calls a day at 3 minutes each, 22 days a month = 3,300 minutes. At $0.10 per minute, that is $330 a month. If your real volume is half that, it is about $165.

To estimate your minutes, check your phone: how many calls come in per day and how long each lasts on average. Most businesses overestimate their volume: measure one week before getting quotes.

The question that matters: what does not answering cost

The agent's price is compared against the cost of the missed call, not against zero. Every unanswered call is a customer calling the next business. Does the per-minute price include everything?

It depends on the platform. Vapi and Retell charge a base plus pass-through for voice and model, with telephony separate: the typical total lands between $0.08 and $0.15 per minute. Bland AI offers a bundled price of about $0.09. Always ask for the all-in total before comparing.

Are there hidden costs beyond the per-minute rate?

The three to check: the platform's monthly subscription (ElevenLabs charges one), the phone number and telephony minutes, and the initial build cost ($600 to $3,000). A good provider shows all of them in writing.

How many minutes does a typical small business use?

Most land between 500 and 2,000 minutes a month, or $50 to $300 monthly all-in. Measure your real calls for one week before getting quotes: almost everyone overestimates their volume.

Does the price drop with higher minute volume?

In practice the per-minute cost stays fairly stable; the real savings come from picking the right platform and not paying for premium setups your case does not need. High volume is negotiated directly with the provider.
