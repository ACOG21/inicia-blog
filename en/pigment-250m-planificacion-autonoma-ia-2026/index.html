Pigment Raises $250M at a $2.3B Valuation: Autonomous Business Planning Has Started | inicIA

By inicIA · October 22, 2026 · 6 min read

Direct answer

On October 21, 2026, Pigment — the business planning and performance management platform — announced a $250 million Series E at a $2.3 billion valuation, led by EQT's Scaleup Europe Fund, with participation from World Innovation Lab, Greenoaks, ICONIQ, IVP and Meritech, plus a planned NVIDIA investment (PRNewswire, PYMNTS, Pulse2). The capital accelerates Pigment's mission: autonomous planning for global enterprises, with AI agents running planning workflows end to end. The announcement's data: Pigment crossed $100 million in annual recurring revenue in 2026 (doubling for the second year running) and today 85% of new metrics created on the platform are generated by an AI agent. Customers: Danone, Siemens, Unilever, Snowflake, ServiceNow, Anthropic, ElevenLabs, Uber — and L'Oréal, which is replacing its salary planning tool for more than 95,000 employees with Pigment.

What the Market Is Buying With These $250M

  • Graphite, the compute engine: thousands of AI agents and people working on the same governed model, with hundreds of billions of data points, deterministic and fully traceable results. "Agents can only be trusted to run planning workflows and act on the results when every number they produce is accurate and every action can be traced" (Pigment's release).
  • The leap from "assisting" to "executing": from the Modeler Agent (builds models from natural-language instructions) to the upcoming Planner Agent, which automates the full workflow — generating forecasts, running scenarios, updating plans.
  • "Enterprise planning will change more in the next few years than it has in the last 30" (Eléonore Crespo and Romain Niccoli, co-founders). Legacy tools — read: spreadsheets and old suites — are being left behind.
  • The seal of those who know AI best: Anthropic and ElevenLabs, which could build this internally, choose to buy Pigment as the "reasoning runtime" for their enterprise data. That says a lot about the product's maturity.

What It Means for Your Business (Even If You're Not Siemens)

  1. The agentic CFO is a matter of time: if your budget lives today in Excel with email versions, you're on the obsolete side of this story. The question now is who in your industry adopts them first.
  2. Traceability before autonomy: the key line of the announcement is the condition — every number exact, every action traceable. Before letting an agent touch your finances, demand that standard from any vendor. Without traceability, there is no trustworthy autonomy.
  3. Your data has to be ready: Pigment works because its customers have organized enterprise data. The prior work is the same as ever: define what gets measured, who decides, how it gets updated. AI doesn't plan on top of chaos.
  4. It competes with Microsoft, SAP, IBM and Anaplan: the round validates the entire category. If you're evaluating planning tools, include agents in the evaluation — 2027's prices and capabilities won't look like today's.

Frequently asked questions

What is Pigment?

A Paris-based (founded 2019) business planning and performance management platform competing with Microsoft, SAP, IBM and Anaplan, with its own compute engine (Graphite) and AI agents that automate planning.

Who led the round and how much did they raise?

$250 million in Series E at a $2.3 billion valuation, led by the Scaleup Europe Fund (managed by EQT), with World Innovation Lab, Greenoaks, ICONIQ, IVP, Meritech and a planned NVIDIA investment.

What is "autonomous planning"?

AI agents that run planning workflows from start to finish: generating forecasts, running scenarios and updating plans automatically, with deterministic, traceable results.

Why should I care if my business is small?

Because it marks where the category is heading: static spreadsheet budgets are being replaced by live models with agents. Adopting accessible versions of that discipline (organized data, updated forecasts) puts you on the right side before it becomes mandatory.
