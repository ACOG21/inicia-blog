AegisAI Launched Proteus: AI vs AI in Your Inbox (and How to Read Its 99% Claim) | inicIA

By inicIA · October 19, 2026 · 6 min read

Direct answer

On October 8, 2026, startup AegisAI announced Proteus: an artificial intelligence model built specifically for email security. Instead of filtering with static rules, Proteus investigates each message —sender identity, embedded links, requested action— before it reaches your inbox. The company claims it detected more than 99% of 3,020 human-reviewed phishing attacks in its internal benchmark, versus 81% for an unnamed leading closed-source general-purpose model and 80% for an unnamed open-weight model. That figure is self-reported and has not been independently audited. Behind the announcement is a serious thesis: AI-crafted attacks now bypass traditional controls at nearly twice the rate of human-written ones, so the defense has to be AI too. AegisAI has raised $49 million ($36M Series A on July 23, 2026 led by Battery Ventures, after a $13M seed in Sep 2025 with Accel and Foundation Capital).

Why this matters for your business

  • The attack changed its nature: AegisAI's study presented at the 2026 M3AAWG conference, covering more than 20,000 phishing and scam emails, found that AI-generated spear phishing grew from 2.8% to 13.9% of all observed phishing in a single year.
  • Email is the front line: the FBI's 2025 Internet Crime Report recorded record losses of $20.8 billion, with business email compromise (BEC) accounting for $11.64 billion —versus under $52 million for ransomware and malware combined.
  • The tactic is personalization: attackers use language models to research your employee, map their professional relationships, and write a tailored lure. "They've researched you, they understand everything about you," CEO Cy Khormaee summarized.

What Proteus promises to do differently

  • Analyze intent, not signatures: instead of matching known patterns, the system evaluates the intent behind a message —unusual sender behavior, subtle language deviations, metadata that doesn't add up.
  • Browse like a human: its companion agent, Vanguard (announced in March), follows suspicious links and attachments onto the open web and returns a threat report within minutes.
  • Integrate without friction: it connects to Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace via API, with no MX record changes required.

The honest part: how to read the "99%"

The figure compares the specialized model against two unnamed general-purpose models —a comparison favorable by design. Also, AegisAI disclosed no pricing or customer count for Proteus (at the company's 2025 public launch it reported three paying customers: Lokker, Mesh Connect and others). None of this invalidates the product; it simply sets how to evaluate any AI security promise:

  1. Ask for independent benchmarks: self-reported figures are the starting point, not the proof.
  2. Ask about your false positives: 99% detection is useless if it blocks legitimate customer emails.
  3. Measure against your real threat: if your team receives generic lures, a traditional filter is enough; if they move money by email, you need agentic defense.
  4. The human link remains: no tool eliminates the need for verification protocols on transfers and sensitive requests.
