Google launched Gemini 4 Hydrogen: the fast model for coding and agents, at $1.50 per million tokens

By inicIA · November 13, 2026 · 6 min read

Direct answer

On November 12, 2026, Google introduced Gemini 4 Hydrogen, the fast member of the Gemini 4 family for everyday coding and agentic workflows. It costs $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens, flat across its full 1-million-token context window. On the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index it scores 51 at its default high thinking level and 54 at the new xhigh level, at a cost of $1.87 per task at high, against $5.48 for Gemini 4 Carbon. It is available from today in the Gemini API, Gemini for business, the Gemini app, Google Search and for paid Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers in Antigravity.

What Hydrogen brings and how it is priced

  • Four thinking levels: low, medium, high by default and a new xhigh tier. Each level tunes quality, latency and cost in a smooth, predictable progression, according to Google.
  • Flat pricing across 1 million tokens: $1.50 input and $7.50 output per million, with no surcharge for long prompts across the whole context window. Cached input costs $0.075 per million tokens.
  • Intelligence score 51 at high and 54 at xhigh: on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, Hydrogen beats Argon at its default high setting (51 against 50) and reaches 54 at xhigh, completing tasks faster than other models in its class.
  • Built for multi-model workloads: Google presents it as the execution model inside the Gemini 4 family, alongside the frontier models Carbon and Argon, so teams can mix models by task.

What changes for your business

  • Cost per task is the number that rules: $1.87 per task at the high level, against $5.48 for Carbon. For daily coding and agent work, the difference adds up across hundreds of tasks a month.
  • Thinking levels are a spending control: the low level handles routine work with fewer tokens and xhigh stays for work that demands more reasoning. The team that picks the level per task spends less than the team that runs everything on high.
  • Flash pricing rises in January: the promotional Gemini Flash rate ($0.75 input and $3.75 output) ends December 31, 2026 and moves to $1.50 and $7.50 on January 1, 2027, matching Hydrogen. The price comparison changes with the year.
  • Availability is broad from day one: API, app, Search, Gemini for business and Antigravity. The team already using Gemini can test Hydrogen in its current workflow.

What to do in your business this week

  • Run your repeated task on Hydrogen and on your current model: measure tokens, time and quality on the same task. The per-task number is the one that decides the switch.
  • Set the thinking level by task type: routine on low or medium, complex on high, critical on xhigh. A written rule in the workflow beats each person's intuition.
  • Review your Flash bill before January: the Flash rate change on January 1, 2027 changes the cost of what runs cheap today. Calculate the impact with your real volumes.
  • Ask your provider for the per-task breakdown: if your agent runs on the Gemini API, the report must show model, thinking level and tokens per task. Control starts with that breakdown.

Note: this article summarizes Google's announcement of November 12, 2026 and coverage in AgentsPulse and The Weighted Average. The index scores and per-task costs are from Artificial Analysis. Google reports token reductions of 12% to 23% in specific partner workflows. The model's knowledge cutoff is March 2026.
