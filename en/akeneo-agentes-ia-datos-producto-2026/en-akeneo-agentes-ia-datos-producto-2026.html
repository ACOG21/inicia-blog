Akeneo put AI agents to work on product data: the catalog is the new storefront

By inicIA · November 3, 2026 · 6 min read

Direct answer

On October 28, 2026, Akeneo announced its Autumn Release 2026: its agentic orchestrator Ziggy now works with Shopify to help prepare and publish product data to online stores, inside an AI harness that applies the same role-based permissions as the human team, records every action as a traceable change and proposes changes a person approves before they are applied. The release adds Readiness Policies, Product Compatibility, enterprise access governance and new Supplier Data Manager capabilities, alongside the acquisition of Dataggo, an AI-native PIM integration specialist.

What the release brings, piece by piece

  • Ziggy for Shopify: the connector ships in two editions: Standard, for up to three stores, and Enterprise, for organizations with many stores. Availability of each capability varies by Product Cloud edition.
  • Readiness Policies: teams define what it means for a product to be ready for each channel, market or objective, combining rules on values, categories, descriptions and relationships. Quality stops being measured by complete fields alone.
  • Product Compatibility: fitment rules that consistently link products, spare parts and accessories, instead of relying on hand-maintained spreadsheets.
  • Supplier Data Manager: Supplier Collaboration and Supplier Catalog Management turn supplier contributions into structured data continuously, with SSO and MFA added on security.
  • Product Model Suggestions: AI-assisted suggestions for grouping products and ordering the catalog with structure, repeatability and governance.
  • PX Insights and AI labeling: analysis of how products are discovered and how ready they are to be found by AI is automated, and transparent labeling identifies what an agent wrote and what a person validated.

What it means for a business selling online

  • Your catalog is your salesperson to AI: buying agents and AI search compare products on structured data, not promises. An incomplete or inconsistent catalog drops out before competing on price.
  • Governance is the difference between agent and risk: role-based permissions, traceable changes and prior human approval are the model Deloitte's report calls for in any permissioned agent. Akeneo applies it to the catalog.
  • The bottleneck is the supplier: much of commerce's manual work lives in receiving, cleaning and validating supplier data. Automating that intake frees the team to sell.
  • Start with your top 100 products: define readiness for your main channel, measure how many products pass the rule today, and raise that percentage before expanding the catalog.

Note: this article summarizes Akeneo's October 28, 2026 release and PR Newswire, Unite.AI and Retail Times coverage. Availability of each capability varies by Product Cloud edition, and some features are described as beta in the cited documentation.
