Adobe put its customer experience agent inside ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini: the agent lives where you already work

By inicIA · November 5, 2026 · 6 min read

Direct answer

On November 4, 2026, Adobe announced that its CX Enterprise Coworker now runs inside ChatGPT, Claude, Databricks Genie, Gemini for Business and Microsoft Copilot, with AWS, IBM and Snowflake integrations on the way, with no timeline given. The agent reached general availability in June 2026, is built on MCP and Agent2Agent (A2A), and runs customer experience orchestration workflows on Adobe CX Enterprise with human oversight: campaign performance analysis, audience forecasting, journey optimization, conflict resolution, and planning and work management. Adobe named nine global systems integrators and six agencies already building on the agent.

The numbers that matter

  • 5 launch platforms: ChatGPT as a plugin, Claude, Databricks Genie, Gemini for Business and Microsoft Copilot; 3 more on the way.
  • 9 global integrators: Accenture, Capgemini, Cognizant, Deloitte Digital, EY, IBM, Infosys, PwC and TCS.
  • 6 agencies: Dentsu, Havas, Omnicom, Publicis, Stagwell and WPP.
  • 42.7% prefer agents: in Futurum's second-half 2026 survey (833 IT decision-makers), 42.7% prefer generative AI delivered mainly through autonomous agents, against 31.2% who prefer copilots.
  • Available since June 2026: the agent was already general before this integration; what is new is that it lives inside the assistants your team already uses.

What changes for your business

  • The agent goes to the assistant, not the other way around: your team asks in ChatGPT or Claude and Adobe's agent runs the workflow in the customer experience platform, without switching windows.
  • Adoption is decided in daily work: buyers deploy agents first in IT operations and cybersecurity, not in customer-facing work, per Futurum's survey. Start with one measurable internal workflow.
  • Open standards are the door: MCP and A2A are what let one agent live on five platforms. Ask your vendors to speak those protocols.
  • The open questions stay open: Adobe did not disclose pricing, how actions taken from third-party assistants are governed and audited, or whether features are the same across all five platforms. Ask for those answers in writing before deploying.

Note: this article summarizes Adobe's November 4, 2026 announcement and Futurum Group's analysis. The covered features and partner list come from the announcement; deployment preferences come from Futurum's survey (second half of 2026, 833 respondents). Adobe did not disclose pricing or cross-platform governance details.
